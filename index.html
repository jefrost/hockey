How these maps work
  • Baseline: the rest of the league (games didn't play), or, with a player selected, his comparison group without him.
  • Measure: attempts per 60 minutes of ice time, or each area's share of the half's attempts.
  • Colour strength shows how sure we are a difference is real. Outlined = beyond chance at 95% confidence. Each area is tested separately, so about 1 in 20 outlines can be luck. Areas with very few attempts stay faint.
  • Smoothing: attempts are spread over nearby areas, allowing for shots clustering within games.
